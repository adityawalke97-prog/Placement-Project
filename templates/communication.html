Interview Communication Practice

Prepare answers for frequently asked HR questions.

01

Tell me about yourself.

Start with your education, technical skills, project experience and career goal.

💡 Tip: Keep your answer between 60–90 seconds.
02

Why should we hire you?

Talk about your skills, willingness to learn, problem-solving ability and project experience.

💡 Tip: Connect your skills with the job requirements.
03

What are your strengths?

Mention qualities such as teamwork, communication, learning ability and consistency.

💡 Tip: Give one real example with your answer.
04

What is your weakness?

Mention a genuine weakness and explain what you are doing to improve it.

💡 Tip: Never say "I have no weaknesses."
